# Aloha Build Club — Founding Cohort Go-To-Market Kit

*For filling the first cohort. Goal: 10 paying founding members. Validation logic from the aloha-ai brief (payment = the only real signal).*

---

## Part 1 — Validation interview questions (run 8–10 before you sell)

Talk to 8–10 people in your target wedge *before* pushing the paid link. You're listening for one repeated phrase — that phrase becomes your headline. Keep it a conversation, not a survey. Don't pitch; diagnose.

**Warm-up / context**
1. "Walk me through the last time you sat down to build something with AI. What were you making, and what actually happened?"
2. "How many things are you currently in the middle of building but haven't shipped? What's the oldest one?"

**The pain (let them talk — this is the gold)**
3. "When you stall on one of these, what's the actual moment it dies? Is it starting, getting stuck mid-way, or losing steam before you publish?"
4. "What have you already tried to fix that? (courses, tools, communities, accountability buddies, nothing?)"
5. "What did those cost you, and why did or didn't they work?"

**The mechanism test**
6. "If you had a set time every weekday where you showed up and built alongside other people — cameras optional — with an AI that handed you your next step, would that change anything for you? Why / why not?"
7. "What would have to be true for you to actually show up more than twice?"

**Willingness to pay (ask plainly — don't flinch)**
8. "If that room existed and it genuinely got you shipping, what would feel fair to pay per month?"
9. "Would you rather pay monthly or annually? What price would make you hesitate?"

**Close**
10. "If I opened a founding cohort in the next couple weeks at a locked-in rate, would you want one of the first seats? Can I hold one for you?"

> **Read the signal:** you're looking for (a) the same stall-point named 3+ times, (b) people who've *already paid* to fix this, and (c) at least 5 unprompted "yes, hold me a seat." If you get those, open the paid link. If people are lukewarm at #10, the wedge or price is wrong — fix that before building anything.

## Part 2 — 10 outreach DMs (LinkedIn, per your cold-DM playbook)

Rules baked in: short, specific, no pitch in message 1, one clear ask, UTM on any link. Standard link tag: `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=dm&utm_campaign=build-club-founding`. Personalize the `[bracket]` bits — never send raw.

### A. Diagnostic-first (for interviews — send these to the first ~15 people)

**DM 1 — the peer who posts about half-built projects**
> Hey [name] — saw your post about [the AI thing you're tinkering with]. Totally unrelated to any pitch: I'm talking to a handful of people who build with AI about where they actually stall out. Would you be up for a 15-min call this week? Genuinely just research, no ask.

**DM 2 — the "I have 6 tabs open" builder**
> [name], quick one — you mentioned you've got a few AI projects going at once. I'm mapping why builds stall between "cool idea" and "shipped." Could I steal 15 min of your brain? I'll send you what I learn from everyone.

**DM 3 — the ex-colleague / warm tie**
> Hey [name]! Working on something new and want to get it right before I build it. You've always been someone who actually ships — can I ask you a few questions about how you stay consistent building with AI? 15 min, this week?

**DM 4 — the creator who went quiet**
> [name] — noticed you were posting [content type] and then it went quiet (been there). I'm researching what makes people stall on creating and what would actually get them back in rhythm. Would love your honest take — 15 min?

**DM 5 — the founder using AI as their whole team**
> Hi [name] — solo-building [product] with AI is exactly the person I'm trying to understand right now. I'm researching why solo builders stall and what a daily "build room" would need to be worth showing up to. Open to a short call?

## Part 3 — Week-by-week (from the brief's validation plan)

- **Week 1:** send DMs 1–5 to ~15 people → run 8–10 interviews. Rewrite the headline using their words.
- **Week 2:** run 5 free daily rooms on Zoom for the interviewees. You are the AI co-pilot (hand out next steps live). Test: do they come back? do they ship? do they thank you?
- **Week 3:** open the paid link. Send DMs 6–10 to warm yeses. **Target: 10 paying members.** Payment is the only validation that counts.
- **Week 4:** automate the single biggest friction you felt facilitating (almost always: generating each person's next step). Everything else stays manual until it hurts.
- **Kill/scale gate:** ≥10 payers AND ≥40% attending 3+×/week → scale. Otherwise fix retention before building any AI features.
